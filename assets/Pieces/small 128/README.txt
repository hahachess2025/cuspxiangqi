Xiangqi Piece and Board Design
Color System, Visual Rationale, and License
Overview

This project presents a modern yet culturally respectful visual design for Xiangqi (Chinese chess).
The goal is to support both Chinese and international players by preserving traditional Chinese notation while adding subtle, secondary English identifiers.

The design emphasizes:

clarity and readability

cultural correctness

visual hierarchy

neutrality of materials

consistency across pieces and board

1. Color System Specification
1.1 Piece Background Colors

Red Side Pieces

Color: #F5EEDC

Description: Warm ivory stone

Black Side Pieces

Color: #EEE8DA

Description: Slightly cooler ivory stone

The difference between red and black piece backgrounds is intentionally subtle.
Side identity is communicated primarily through character color, not material color.

1.2 Piece Characters and English Letters

Red Side

Color: Deep red ink (cinnabar-inspired)

Black Side

Color: Black ink

Typography

Chinese characters use a printed Song-style font

No calligraphic or stylistic reinterpretation

Stroke geometry is consistent across all pieces

English letters are included as secondary labels to support non-Chinese players.

Letter size: approximately 1/3 of the piece height

Placement: above the Chinese character

Purpose: learning aid and quick recognition, not replacement of Chinese notation

1.3 Board Color

Board Surface

Color: #9F9A8C

Description: Ink-wash stone gray

This mid-tone color:

Clearly separates the board from light-colored pieces

Supports both red and black ink

Avoids visual competition with piece characters

Provides a calm, neutral playing surface

1.4 Grid and Structural Lines

Grid lines, borders, and palace diagonals

Color: Black

Line weight: slightly heavier than coordinate markings

These elements provide structural clarity and remain readable at both close and distant viewing scales.

1.5 Rank and File Coordinates

Coordinate markings

Color: #6F6B60

Description: Soft charcoal gray

Coordinates are designed to be:

readable when referenced

visually quiet during play

subordinate to pieces and grid lines

2. Design Rationale
2.1 Clarity Over Ornamentation

All visual decisions prioritize gameplay clarity.
Decorative elements are avoided in favor of clean geometry and controlled contrast.

2.2 Cultural Accuracy and Accessibility

Traditional Chinese characters remain the primary identifiers

Correct character forms are used for each side and piece

English letters are secondary and unobtrusive

This dual-label system allows:

Chinese players to play naturally

International players to learn and recognize pieces without replacing traditional notation

2.3 Soft Forms, System-Level Contrast

Pieces are intentionally designed with:

rounded edges

subtle surface noise

no hard outlines

Because of this, contrast is achieved through system-level color relationships, not heavy borders or outlines.

2.4 The Board as a Stage

The board is visually supportive rather than dominant.

Its role is to:

organize space

guide movement

recede behind the pieces

The pieces and their characters remain the visual focus at all times.
